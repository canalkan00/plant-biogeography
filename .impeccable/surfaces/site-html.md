# Surface: lab website (single page)

Scope: index.html / site.html, the whole public site. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: prospective MSc/PhD students and collaborating researchers, often on phones. Action: understand the lab's focus and e-mail the lab (barisoz@hacettepe.edu.tr). Content: about, study groups, publications, team (6 confirmed people), contact. Constraints: TR/EN with equal weight, mobile-first, no photographs yet, no invented student topics.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The lab reads plant history from landscape, so the page is a relief map of Anatolia's mountains, not a university template. It refuses the centred hero + card grid lab page.

OWN-WORLD: Hypsometric tint palette: lowland green, steppe ochre, alpine pale stone, deep relief-shadow ink, one mustard line for the Anatolian Diagonal. Contour linework, wide-set map lettering for region-scale headings, small mono coordinates. Navigation is a map legend; buttons are legend keys.

STORY: The visitor sees a living contour field crossed by the Anatolian Diagonal and learns in one line that the lab studies Brassicaceae across Anatolia; scrolls through study groups pinned like map annotations, publications, the team; writes an e-mail.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed canvas contour field (slowly drifting isolines in tint bands). Mustard dashed Diagonal crossing corner to corner. Left/top: lab name in wide map lettering at display scale, one-line thesis, two actions (Research, Contact e-mail). Bottom edge: legend strip with coordinates 39.87° N 32.73° E and TR/EN switch in the header.

FORM: Relief map (grounded list position 4 of 7). seed:c3af99021abc4b64. Degraded roll, no challengers.

Signature interaction: contour field reacts to pointer/touch (isolines lift around the finger); motion grammar: slow drift, nothing bounces; reduced-motion renders a still map.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
