## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS in a single `index.html`, served by GitHub Pages from `main` (`canalkan00/plant-biogeography`). `site.html` is the same page without the document skeleton, used for the Claude artifact preview. No build step.

## Users

- **Prospective MSc and PhD students** deciding whether to apply to the lab: they want to know what the lab studies, who works there, and how to get in touch.
- **Researchers and potential collaborators** in Türkiye and abroad: they want the research focus, study groups and publications, then a contact route.

Many visitors arrive on a phone (links shared by message or from a conference), so mobile use is a first-class requirement.

## Product Purpose

The public website of the Plant Biogeography Lab at Hacettepe University. It explains what the lab studies, shows its research and publications, introduces the team, and gives a way to get in touch. Success: a prospective student or collaborator leaves knowing the lab's focus and writes an e-mail.

## Positioning

The lab studies the evolution, diversity and distribution of Brassicaceae (the mustard family) in Anatolia, where the Mediterranean, Caucasus and Irano-Anatolian biodiversity hotspots meet. Methods: field collection, herbarium records, molecular phylogenetics, phylogeography and ecological niche modelling.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Bilingual: every piece of copy exists in Turkish and English with a visible language switch; the choice is remembered per visitor.
- Must work well on phones (navigation, tap targets, readable type, no horizontal scroll).
- Sections: About, Research, Publications, People, Contact.
